## Pricing

PRVT Market uses platform-set pricing for each company listing.

- You buy at the price shown
- You sell back to PRVT Market at the price shown
- You do not enter your own price

## Availability

Availability depends on the platform inventory.

- When users buy, inventory decreases
- When users sell, inventory increases immediately
