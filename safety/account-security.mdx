## Protect your account

- Use a strong, unique password [Learn more](/safety/strong-password)
- Don’t share your password with anyone
- Only sign in through official PRVT Market pages

## Login attempt alerts

For every login attempt, PRVT Market sends an account access alert email to the account owner’s email address.

If you recognize the login attempt, you can ignore the email.

If you do not recognize the login attempt, secure your account immediately.

### What the alert email includes

The login alert email can include details such as:

- Device
- Browser
- Location
- Time
- Account type
- IP address

### What to do if it wasn’t you

- Reset your PRVT Market password immediately
- Make sure your new password is strong and unique
- Check that your email account is secure
- Contact [support](mailto:hello@prvtmarket.com) and report the suspicious login attempt

## Suspicious activity

If you suspect unauthorized activity, contact [support](mailto:hello@prvtmarket.com) immediately.
