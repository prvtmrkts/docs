## Do I need to deposit before buying?

Yes. You must deposit funds before you can place a buy order.

## Can I choose my own buy or sell price?

No. PRVT Market uses platform-set pricing for each listing. You buy and sell at the prices shown on the platform.

## Who do I sell my shares to?

You sell shares back to PRVT Market.

## Is selling always available?

Yes. Selling is always available.

## Is there a commission on selling?

No. PRVT Market does not charge a commission on selling.

## What happens after I click Buy or Sell?

You receive an instant confirmation and your portfolio updates immediately.

## Why does the available inventory change?

When you buy shares, available inventory decreases. When you sell shares, those shares return to platform inventory immediately.
