## Do I need to deposit before buying?

Yes. You must deposit funds before you can place a buy order.

## Can I choose my own buy or sell price?

No. PRVT Market uses platform-set pricing for each listing. You buy and sell at the prices shown on the platform.

## Who do I sell my shares to?

You sell shares back to PRVT Market.

## Is selling always available?

Yes. Selling is always available.

## Is there a commission on selling?

No. PRVT Market does not charge a commission on selling.

## What happens after I click Buy or Sell?

You receive an instant confirmation and your portfolio updates immediately.

## Why does the available inventory change?

When you buy shares, available inventory decreases. When you sell shares, those shares return to platform inventory immediately.

## Are SPV and BTC investments the same as buying shares?

No. SPV and BTC investments follow a product lifecycle with lock and maturity behavior.

See:

- [SPV investments](/trading/spv-investments)
- [BTC investments](/trading/btc-investments)
- [Investment lifecycle](/trading/investment-lifecycle)

## How do fees work for SPV and BTC investments?

SPV and BTC products can use fee models that differ from standard share trades. Your net invested amount is calculated after applicable fees.

See [Fees](/funding/fees).

## Why is my investment still locked?

Locked means your position is still in its lifecycle before final redemption. Timing can vary by product and processing windows.

If your status appears delayed, contact support with your account email, product name, investment amount, and approximate time.
