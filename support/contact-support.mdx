## Email support

For help, email:

[hello@prvtmarket.com](mailto:hello@prvtmarket.com)

## What to include

To help resolve your issue quickly, include:

- Your account email
- What you were trying to do (deposit, buy, sell, withdraw)
- Company name (if applicable)
- Number of shares (if applicable)
- Approximate time of the issue
- Any screenshots or error messages
