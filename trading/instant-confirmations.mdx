## What to expect

PRVT Market provides instant confirmations for buys and sells.

After you confirm a buy or sell:

- Your portfolio updates immediately
- Your account balances update immediately
- Inventory updates immediately

## If something looks wrong

If a confirmation succeeds but your portfolio does not reflect the change, contact support with:

- Your account email
- Company name
- Number of shares
- Approximate time of the trade
