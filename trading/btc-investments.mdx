## Overview

BTC investments let you allocate funds to a BTC product on PRVT Market.

Your investment is shown in your account with status updates over its lockup lifecycle.

## Before you invest

- You must have available USDC in your PRVT wallet.
- The BTC product must be open.
- Your investment amount must be within product minimum and maximum limits.

## How your amount is processed

When you submit a BTC investment:

1. You enter a gross amount.
2. Any upfront fee is deducted.
3. The remaining amount is recorded as net invested.

The net invested amount is used as your principal basis for maturity calculations.

## Lockup and maturity timing

BTC products include a lockup period.

During lockup:

- Your position remains locked.
- Funds are not yet redeemed.

After lockup ends, maturity processing can move your position to redeemed with a payout.

## Status lifecycle (user view)

Typical BTC lifecycle states are:

- Locked: investment is active during lockup.
- Redeemed: maturity payout has been completed.

Depending on support operations, you may also see refund outcomes when applicable.

## Maturity payout

At maturity, payout can include:

- Principal (based on net invested)
- Yield (if applicable for the product)

Your history may display principal and yield as part of final payout details.

## Where to check your activity

Use your portfolio or activity history to track:

- Initial BTC investment event
- BTC maturity event when redemption completes
- Final payout values when posted

## If something looks delayed

If the lockup period has passed and your status has not updated, contact support with:

- Your account email
- Product name
- Investment amount
- Approximate time of investment

See [Contact support](/support/contact-support).
