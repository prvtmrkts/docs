---

## Pricing

PRVT Market uses fixed prices for each company listing.

- Prices are set on the platform
- Users do not enter their own buy or sell prices

## Inventory

Each listing has a share inventory available for purchase.

- When a user buys shares, available inventory decreases
- When a user sells shares, those shares return to platform inventory immediately

### Example

If a listing starts with 1,000 shares available:

- A user buys 10 shares → 990 shares remain available
- That user later sells 10 shares → 1,000 shares become available again
