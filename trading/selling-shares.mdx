## Overview

When you sell shares on PRVT Market, you sell them back to the platform at the price currently offered on PRVT Market.

## How to sell

1. Sign in to your PRVT Market account
2. Open your portfolio
3. Select the company you want to sell
4. Enter the number of shares you want to sell
5. Review the estimated proceeds
6. Click **Sell** to confirm

## Confirmation and portfolio updates

After you confirm the sell:

- You receive an instant confirmation
- Your portfolio updates immediately
- Sold shares return to platform inventory immediately and become available for other users to buy

## Fees

PRVT Market does not charge a commission on selling.
