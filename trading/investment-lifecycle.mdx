## Overview

This page explains the common lifecycle states and activity timeline events for SPV and BTC investments.

Use this guide to understand what each status means and what to expect next.

## Lifecycle at a glance

Most investments follow this pattern:

1. You invest.
2. Your position is locked.
3. At maturity, redemption is processed.
4. Your payout is posted and status updates to redeemed.

In some cases, a refund flow may apply.

## Status glossary

### Locked

Your investment is active and currently in its lock period or pending maturity processing.

What this means for you:

- Your funds are committed to the product.
- You are waiting for maturity or a supported resolution path.

### Redeemed

Your maturity redemption has been processed.

What this means for you:

- Your payout has been calculated and posted.
- Your investment lifecycle is complete for that position.

### Refunded

Your investment amount has been returned through a refund flow.

What this means for you:

- The original investment did not continue through normal maturity completion.
- Your account history reflects the refund outcome.

## Activity timeline event types

Your activity timeline can include these SPV and BTC investment events:

- `spv_invest`: recorded when an SPV investment is created.
- `spv_maturity`: recorded when SPV maturity redemption completes.
- `btc_invest`: recorded when a BTC investment is created.
- `btc_maturity`: recorded when BTC maturity redemption completes.

These events help you track the progression from initial investment to final payout.

## Payout terminology

When maturity is completed, payout may include:

- Principal: your net invested base amount.
- Yield: product yield when applicable.
- Total payout: principal plus any applicable yield.

Your account history may present these values as separate fields.

## If a status seems delayed

Status updates can take time depending on processing windows.

If an update seems delayed:

1. Confirm whether the expected maturity window has passed.
2. Check your activity timeline for new investment or maturity events.
3. Contact support if the status still has not changed.

When contacting support, include:

- Your account email
- Product name
- Investment amount
- Approximate time of investment

See [Contact support](/support/contact-support).
