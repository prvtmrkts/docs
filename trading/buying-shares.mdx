## Overview

On PRVT Market, share prices are set on the platform for each company listing. When you buy, you buy at the listed price.

## Before you buy

- You must have deposited funds before placing a buy order.
- Buying is subject to available inventory.

## How to buy

1. Sign in to your PRVT Market account
2. Open the company you want to buy
3. Enter the number of shares you want
4. Review the total cost and any buy commission
5. Click **Buy** to confirm

## Confirmation and portfolio updates

After you confirm the buy:

- You receive an instant confirmation
- Your portfolio updates immediately
- The available inventory shown on the platform decreases by the number of shares you purchased

## Pricing and commissions

- The share price is set on the platform
- A buy commission may apply (shown before confirming)
