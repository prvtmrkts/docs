## Overview

SPV investments let you allocate funds to a specific SPV product on PRVT Market.

After you invest, your position is tracked in your account and appears in your activity timeline.

## Before you invest

- You must have available USDC in your PRVT wallet.
- The SPV product must be open for new investments.
- Your investment must be within the product minimum and maximum limits.

## How your amount is processed

When you submit an SPV investment:

1. You enter a gross amount.
2. Applicable fees are deducted.
3. The remaining amount becomes your net invested amount.

For SPV investments, fees can include:

- Upfront SPV fee
- Platform fee

You can use the net invested amount to understand your effective principal after fees.

## What happens after you confirm

After confirmation:

- Your investment is recorded in your account.
- The position starts in a locked state.
- A timeline activity is created for your SPV investment.

If the investment later reaches maturity redemption, another timeline activity is recorded for the maturity event.

## Status lifecycle (user view)

Typical SPV lifecycle states are:

- Locked: your funds are invested and not yet redeemed.
- Redeemed: payout has been processed at maturity.
- Refunded: funds were returned under a refund flow.

Timing can vary based on product rules and processing windows.

## Maturity and payout

At redemption time, payout can include:

- Principal (your net invested amount)
- Maturity yield (if applicable)

Your account history may show payout and yield amounts separately.

## Where to check your activity

Use your portfolio or activity history to track:

- Initial SPV investment event
- Maturity event when redemption is completed
- Final payout values when available

## If something looks delayed

If your status or timeline has not updated as expected, contact support with:

- Your account email
- Product name
- Investment amount
- Approximate time of investment

See [Contact support](/support/contact-support).
