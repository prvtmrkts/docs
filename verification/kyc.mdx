## Overview

PRVT Market uses **[Bridge](https://bridge.xyz)** to manage identity verification (KYC) and Terms of Service (TOS).

To trade (buy/sell), you must:

1. Have an active PRVT Market account
2. Accept Bridge Terms of Service
3. Complete KYC verification

## Before you start

- You must have an account on PRVT Market.
- If you don't have one yet, sign up here:
  [Create an account](https://prvtmarket.com/en/auth/signup)

<Info>We recommend completing KYC verification after signup to get full access to PRVT Market resources.</Info>

## Start verification

After your first login, you may be prompted to complete KYC onboarding.

To begin, go to:

[KYC Verification](https://prvtmarket.com/en/kyc)

On this page, PRVT Market generates your Bridge verification links using your account **full name** and **email**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept Terms of Service">
    Review and accept the Bridge Terms of Service. This step is required before verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete KYC verification">
    The KYC flow includes the following steps:

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Basic information">
        Provide the following details:

        - Country
        - First name
        - Middle name (optional)
        - Last name
        - Date of birth
        - National ID number
        - Address
      </Step>
      <Step title="Document verification">
        Scan a valid identity document. Supported types include:

        - National ID
        - Driving license
        - Passport
      </Step>
      <Step title="Live face verification">
        Complete a live face scan to match your identity document.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Results: Approved or Rejected

After you complete verification, Bridge returns a final result:

- **Approved**
- **Rejected**

PRVT Market receives your verification status in real time.

<Warning>
  Sometimes KYC may not be approved on the first attempt. Please retry and ensure you are providing valid documents that are clearly visible when scanning.
</Warning>

### If approved

Once your KYC is approved and TOS is accepted, you can access PRVT Market resources such as trading (buy/sell).

In addition, a custodial wallet and a virtual account are created automatically.

<Tip>Your wallet and virtual account are created once KYC is approved and TOS is accepted.</Tip>

<Note>
  In rare cases, wallet and virtual account creation may fail even if KYC is approved and TOS is accepted. If this happens, please contact us for support at [hello@prvtmarket.com](mailto:hello@prvtmarket.com).
</Note>

### If rejected

If your verification is rejected, you can restart the process at any time from the KYC page.
