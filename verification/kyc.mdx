## Overview

PRVT Market uses **Bridge.xyz** to manage identity verification (KYC) and Terms of Service (TOS).

To trade (buy/sell), you must:

1. Have an active PRVT Market account
2. Accept Bridge Terms of Service
3. Complete KYC verification

## Before you start

- You must have an account on PRVT Market.
- If you don’t have one yet, sign up here:
  [Create an account](https://prvtmarket.com/en/auth/signup)

## Start verification

After your first login, you may be prompted to complete KYC onboarding.

To complete verification, go to:

[KYC verification](https://prvtmarket.com/en/kyc)

On this page, PRVT Market generates your Bridge verification links using your account **full name** and **email**.

## Step 1: Accept Terms of Service

You’ll be asked to review and accept Bridge Terms of Service. This step is required before verification.

## Step 2: Complete KYC verification

The KYC flow includes the following categories.

### 1) Basic information

You provide:

- Country
- First name
- Middle name (optional)
- Last name
- Birth date
- National ID number
- Address

### 2) Document verification

You’ll scan an identity document. Supported document types include:

- National ID
- Driving license
- Passport

### 3) Live face verification

You’ll complete a live face scan to match your identity document and help keep verification secure.

## Results: approved or rejected

After you complete verification, Bridge returns a final result:

- Approved
- Rejected

PRVT Market receives your verification status in real time.

### If approved

Once your KYC is approved and TOS is accepted, you can access PRVT Market resources such as trading (buy/sell).

In addition, a custodial wallet and a virtual account are created automatically.

### If rejected

If your verification is rejected, you can restart the verification process at any time from the KYC page.
