## Overview

PRVT Market is a marketplace for buying and selling shares of private companies.

## Who it’s for

- Investors looking to access private company shares
- Shareholders looking to sell private company shares

## What you can do on PRVT Market

- Discover companies and available opportunities
- Place buy and sell orders
- Track orders and holdings

## How trading works (high level)

1. Create an account and complete verification
2. Review a company and decide to buy or sell
3. Place an order
4. If your order matches, the trade executes
5. The trade settles and your holdings update

## Important considerations

Private company shares can involve restrictions, limited liquidity, and higher risk compared to public markets. Make sure you understand the eligibility requirements and trading restrictions before placing trades.
