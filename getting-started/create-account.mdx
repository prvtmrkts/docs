## Create your account

1. Open PRVT Market and click **[Sign up](https://prvtmarket.com/en/auth/signup)**
2. Enter your:
   - Full name
   - Email
   - Password
   - Confirm password
3. Click **Sign up**

<Info>We recommend using a strong password to sign up. This will help keep your account secure. Want to know how to create a strong password? Check out our [Password strength guide](/safety/strong-password).</Info>
If your passwords do not match, you’ll be prompted to fix them before you can continue.

<Warning>Please use a valid email address to sign up. You will receive a verification email to activate your account.</Warning>

## Sign up with Google

Alternatively, you can select **Signup with Google** and follow the Google sign-in flow.

## After you sign up

After a successful sign-up:

- You’ll see a confirmation message in the app
- You can select **Back to login** to sign in to your new account

## Verify your email

After signing up, PRVT Market sends an email verification link to your inbox.

You must click the verification link to activate your account before you can sign in.

## Complete verification

After creating your account, you’ll be prompted to complete verification. This helps keep the marketplace safe and compliant.

## First login: KYC onboarding

After your first login, you’ll be redirected to **[Onboarding KYC](https://prvtmarket.com/en/onboarding/kyc)**.

At this stage, you can:

- Start KYC (recommended)
- Skip and complete KYC later

## Next steps

- Complete verification
- Fund your account (if required before trading)
- Start browsing companies and placing orders
