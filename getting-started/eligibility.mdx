## Who can use PRVT Market?

Eligibility depends on your location and applicable regulatory requirements.

## What you may need

- A verified account (identity verification)
- Additional checks depending on your profile and jurisdiction

## Restrictions

Access to certain companies, listings, or actions may be restricted based on:

- Location/jurisdiction
- Account verification status
- Compliance requirements

## If you can’t complete onboarding

If you’re blocked during onboarding, contact support with the email on your account so the team can help.
