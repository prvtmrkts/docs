## Overview

PRVT Market may apply fees depending on the action you take.

## Buying commission

When you buy shares, PRVT Market charges a commission set for that share listing.

### Example

If a share is priced at $5 and the buy commission is 1%:

- Buying 10 shares costs $50
- Commission is $0.50
- Total is $50.50

## Selling commission

PRVT Market does not charge a commission on selling shares.

## Where to see fees

The commission (if applicable) is shown before you confirm a buy.
