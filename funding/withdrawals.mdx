## Overview

Withdrawals allow you to move available funds out of PRVT Market.

## When funds are available

Your available balance can change immediately after buying or selling shares.

## How to withdraw

1. Sign in to your PRVT Market account
2. Open your wallet or funding page
3. Choose **Withdraw**
4. Enter the amount and confirm

## Troubleshooting

If a withdrawal fails or is delayed, contact support and include:

- Your account email
- Withdrawal amount
- Approximate time of request
