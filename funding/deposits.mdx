## Overview

You must deposit funds before you can place a buy order on PRVT Market.

## When funds are available

Deposits are available to use for buying shares once they appear in your account balance.

## How to deposit

1. Sign in to your PRVT Market account
2. Open your wallet or funding page
3. Choose **Deposit**
4. Follow the instructions to complete the deposit

## Troubleshooting

If your deposit is not showing in your balance, contact support and include:

- Your account email
- Deposit amount
- Approximate time of deposit
