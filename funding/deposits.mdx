## Overview

You must deposit funds before you can place a buy order on PRVT Market.

PRVT Market currently supports these deposit methods:

<Frame>
    <img src="/images/3-way-deposit.png" alt="3-way deposit" style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }} />
</Frame>

1. Buy crypto with card
2. Transfer crypto from an external wallet to your PRVT Market wallet
3. Deposit via bank

Each method has its own requirements and processing times.

<Note>
Take Important Notes: 
1. Only USDC is supported for deposits. 
2. Supported networks are Base, Ethereum, Optimism, and Arbitrum.
</Note>

## When funds are available

Deposits are available to use for buying shares once they appear in your account balance.

## How to deposit

1. Sign in to your PRVT Market account
2. Open your wallet or funding page
3. Choose **Deposit**
4. Follow the instructions to complete the deposit

### Transfer crypto from an external wallet

If you want to deposit USDC from an external wallet (for example: Binance, Coinbase Wallet, MetaMask), follow the step-by-step guide:

[Transfer crypto (wallet-to-wallet)](/funding/deposit/wallet-to-wallet)

## Troubleshooting

If your deposit is not showing in your balance, contact support and include:

- Your account email
- Deposit amount
- Approximate time of deposit
