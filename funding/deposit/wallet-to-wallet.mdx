## Overview

You can deposit funds into PRVT Market by transferring USDC from an external wallet (for example: Binance, Coinbase Wallet, MetaMask) to your PRVT Market deposit address.

## Before you start

- Only deposit **USDC**
- Use a **supported network**
- Always double-check the address and network before sending

## Supported networks

PRVT Market currently supports:

- Base
- Ethereum
- Optimism
- Arbitrum

More EVM networks may be supported in the future.

## Find your deposit address

1. Sign in to PRVT Market
2. Open your wallet
3. Select **Deposit**
4. Select **Transfer crypto**
5. Copy your deposit address (or scan the QR code)

<img src='/images/deposit-address-qr.png' />

## Transfer USDC from an external wallet

1. Open your external wallet or exchange
2. Choose **Send** / **Withdraw**
3. Select **USDC** as the asset
4. Paste your PRVT Market deposit address
5. Select a supported network that matches PRVT Market
6. Confirm and send the transfer

## Deposit instructions and safety checks

We recommend following these checks to avoid loss of funds:

- Confirm you selected **USDC**
- Confirm the destination address matches your PRVT Market deposit address
- Confirm the network matches a supported network
- If it’s your first transfer, consider sending a small test amount first
<img src='/images/deposit-instructions.png' />
<Warning>These instructions are also present on deposit page. Please follow them carefully to prevent loss of funds.</Warning>

## Processing time

Deposits can take a few minutes to process depending on the network.

Once processed, funds appear in your PRVT Market wallet balance immediately.
